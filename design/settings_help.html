FAQs
How does StanDiag work?
StanDiag connects car owners with nearby mechanics. Search for mechanics in your area, view their profiles, and call them directly through the app.
How do credits work?
Credits let you unlock mechanic contact details and run OBD scans. Buy credits in bundles — they never expire so you only pay for what you use.
How do I add a vehicle?
Go to My Vehicles from the side menu and tap the + Add button at the top right. Fill in your vehicle details and save.
What is OBD scanning?
OBD (On-Board Diagnostics) scanning reads your car's internal computer. Connect a Bluetooth OBD adapter to your car's OBD port and tap Scan.
How do I become a mechanic?
Sign up and select Mechanic during registration. Complete your profile with your expertise, workshop address and credentials.
